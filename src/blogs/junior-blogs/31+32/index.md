# junior week may. 15, 2026

went mia after one day at school last week, so i'm just going to include what i did that day onto this post 

## mold
<a href="https://mstricker2608.wixsite.com/mstricker" target="_blank"> matt </a> helped me with the mold by teaching me how it works, and despite how messy everything was, we were able to get it clamped and would be done overnight.

![the mold fully clamped and everything](/static/img/junior/31+32/mold.webp)
and then i was gone for the entire school week after that. came back next week tuesday.

this is what it ended up looking like when it was done. super thin (which is what i wanted), but also too brittle. so it broke and became unsalvagable.

![broken silicone mold results](/static/img/junior/31+32/mold2.webp)

because of that, i decided to just not use the mold and instead just friction fit the pcb with the aluminum case (when it gets made), opting for electrical tape to protect the components
